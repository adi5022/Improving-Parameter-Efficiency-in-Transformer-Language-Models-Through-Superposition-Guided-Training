Relative to the baseline at the best weight decay gamma* (seeds 0-2): m = 15 / 25 / 63 baseline test error 5.82e-4 / 3.37e-4 / 8.53e-5.
- **Overlap:** with lambda >= 1e-2 the learned mean squared overlap equals the Welch minimum at every width (0.0657 / 0.0390 / 0.0149 vs baseline 0.245 / 0.124 / 0.0231). The penalty reaches the theoretical minimum overlap.
- **Test error, plain penalty + gamma*:** m = 15: -2 to -5% (best lambda 1e-2); m = 25: -1% to -10% (lambda 1e-4 .. 1e-1, best 0.1 at -10%); m = 63: -9%, -4%, +0.4%, -15% for lambda 1e-4 .. 1e-1 (not monotone, noisy).
- **Weighted + gamma*:** m = 15: -2 to -5%; m = 25: -3%, -7%, -5%, -15% (lambda 0.1 best); m = 63: -8%, 0%, +18%, -23% (lambda 0.1 best, non-monotone).
- **Penalty alone (gamma = 0):** worse than baseline at gamma* at every width (m = 63, lambda 0.1: +5% with only 81% of features kept; smaller lambda far worse). The penalty does not replace negative weight decay. It does help over gamma = 0 without it.
- **Edge of grid:** the best or near-best lambda is the largest tried (0.1) at m = 25 and 63, so the useful range probably extends above 0.1.
- **Distance to the success mark** (matching L*(1.5 m), i.e. error cut by about 32% / 42% / 46% at m = 15 / 25 / 63): best pilot gains are 5% / 15% / 23%. A real but smaller effect so far.
- **Caveats:** 3 seeds; non-monotone behaviour at m = 63 (and weighted lambda 0.01) says per-cell noise is large; lambda was not yet chosen on separate seeds.

**Proposed next (not run, not approved):** drop the gamma = 0 arm; extend the lambda range upward for the two gamma* arms (lambda 0.3, 1, 3; 54 runs, about 17 min locally); then the final grid on seeds 3-9. Gate thresholds still need confirming before the final runs.
